# Privacy Policy

## What is ReadBear?

ReadBear is a free, open-source application that works entirely offline.

- ReadBear does not collect, transmit, or share any user data.
- The application does not require an internet connection or an account.
- Reading history, bookmarks, library information, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
- No data is sent to the developer or any third-party services.
- ReadBear does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services and telemetry.
- All data remains on your device and under your control.
